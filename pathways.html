02, Pathways

Five directions.
No fixed model.

Select a pathway to examine where it works and what risks require attention. Pathways may overlap, combine or change as local conditions evolve.

Selected pathway

Use What Is Empty

Activate vacant shops, offices, public buildings and industrial sites before demanding new construction.

Works well for

Early stage collectives, visible vacancy, testing demand and cultural actors with limited budgets.

Watch closely

Short term instability, hidden renovation costs, legal uncertainty and gentrification risk.
